Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

We want you to be confident engaging SigOps INC. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for our services.

Cancellation

You may cancel an engagement at any time by emailing dame@sigoperations.net or calling 425-442-6061. Cancellation takes effect when we confirm it in writing.

Pro-rated refunds

If you cancel before the work is complete, you are refunded on a pro-rated basis: you pay for work completed and costs already incurred up to the cancellation date, and we refund the remaining prepaid balance for work not yet performed.

  • Work completed is measured against the milestones or scope in your proposal or invoice.
  • Third-party costs already committed on your behalf (for example licenses, phone numbers, or hosting) are non-refundable.
  • Once a deliverable has been delivered and accepted, that portion is non-refundable.

If something is wrong

If a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, tell us within 14 days of delivery. We will correct it at no charge, and if we cannot, we will refund the affected portion.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Your bank may take additional time to post the credit.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge so we can resolve it quickly.
