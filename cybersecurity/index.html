OUR CORE PROGRAMS

Enterprise Cybersecurity

One AI-monitored security architecture built around your business — not a bundle of disconnected tools, but a single system covering your systems, your data, and your people.

Managed Detection & Response (MDR)

AI models trained on live threat patterns watch your network continuously, flagging and isolating suspicious activity in seconds — before a human analyst could open the alert.

Microsoft 365 Security

Continuous, automated audits of your Microsoft 365 environment — access, permissions, configurations — catching drift the moment it happens, not at the next scheduled review.

Email Protection

AI-based filtering trained on your business's real communication patterns stops phishing, spoofing, and impersonation before it reaches an inbox.

Endpoint Protection

Every laptop, desktop, and device your team uses is monitored in real time, with automated isolation the instant a device behaves abnormally.

Security Awareness Training

Adaptive training that updates as new social engineering tactics emerge, turning your team into an active layer of defense instead of your biggest risk.

Ongoing Monitoring

Dedicated, continuous oversight of your environment — every alert triaged by AI, every anomaly reviewed, every escalation handled before it reaches your desk.

Executive Security Reporting

A clear, plain-language view of your security posture delivered to leadership on a regular cadence — what's being watched, what's been caught, and where you stand.
