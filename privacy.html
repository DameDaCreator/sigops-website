Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Who we are

This website, sigoperation.com, is operated by SigOps Inc., 12819 SE 38th St, #317, Bellevue, WA 98006. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, company, email, phone number and anything you write in our contact form or emails.
  • Billing information: when you pay, your card is processed by our payment processor and gateway (such as Authorize.net). SigOps does not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Service data: call recordings, transcripts and lead information processed through systems we run for clients, handled only to provide the service.
  • Security data: for cybersecurity clients, device, log and network data collected by our monitoring tools, used only to detect and respond to threats and to support your compliance program.
  • Website data: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and IP address, collected by our hosting provider.

How we use it

  • To answer inquiries and provide the services you buy
  • To process payments and send invoices and receipts
  • To send service updates and, if you agree, occasional marketing (you can opt out any time)
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements and prevent fraud

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as payment processors, hosting, email, phone and AI software providers, and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law.

Payment security

Payments are processed over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections by PCI DSS compliant payment providers. Card data is sent directly to the payment gateway and is not stored by SigOps.

Cookies

This site uses only the basic cookies needed to load the website. We do not use advertising cookies on this site.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide services and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete it securely.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails, by contacting dame@sigoperations.net. We respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, and we will honor them.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
