Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Summary

This policy explains when SigOps Inc. ("SigOps," "we," "us") issues refunds and how you can cancel a subscription. Please read it before you buy. By paying for any service you agree to this policy.

Setup fees are non-refundable

Setup fees pay for discovery, configuration, scripting, integration and testing work that begins once payment is received. Setup fees are non-refundable once paid.

The one exception: if SigOps cannot start or deliver the agreed setup work for reasons within our control, we will refund the setup fee in full.

Monthly subscriptions

  • Monthly plans are billed in advance and renew automatically each month on the same date until cancelled.
  • You can cancel any time by giving 30 days written notice by email to dame@sigoperations.net. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the 30 day notice period.
  • Months already billed are not refunded, and we do not give partial-month refunds. Your service keeps running through the end of the notice period.
  • We confirm every cancellation by email within 2 business days.

Annual compliance plans

  • Compliance plans (Sentinel, Guardian, Fortify and Command) are 12-month agreements, paid in full at signing.
  • Compliance plan fees are non-refundable once onboarding begins. If you cancel in writing before onboarding begins, we refund the full amount.
  • The plan cannot be cancelled for a partial refund during the 12-month term. Services continue through the end of the term.
  • If SigOps cannot start or deliver the agreed services for reasons within our control, we refund the unused portion.

Managed security add-ons

Monthly add-on services (EDR/MDR, SIEM/XDR, email domain defense and network monitoring) are billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. Months already billed are not refunded.

Custom work and individual services

Custom builds and one-off services are priced in a written proposal. Any deposit or milestone payment listed in that proposal is non-refundable once the related work has started, unless the proposal says otherwise.

Third-party costs

Ad spend paid to Meta or other platforms, and third-party software fees paid directly by you, are not refundable by SigOps.

Billing errors and disputes

If you think you were charged in error, contact us within 30 days of the charge. If we confirm a billing error, we refund the incorrect amount to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Please contact us before filing a dispute with your card issuer so we can fix it quickly.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
