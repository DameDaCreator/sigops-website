Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

SigOps Inc. is committed to providing professional, reliable technology services and maintaining transparent billing practices. This policy explains how customers may request cancellations, refunds, or adjustments to their purchases.

1. Services Covered

SigOps Inc. provides artificial intelligence solutions, AI voice agents, cybersecurity services, software development, business automation, technology consulting, and related subscription-based services.

As our services are primarily digital and professional in nature, physical product returns generally do not apply.

2. Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of their initial payment.

Refund requests will be reviewed based on the services delivered, project progress, and the terms of the applicable service agreement.

  • Payments for services not yet started may qualify for a full refund.
  • Projects already in development may qualify for a partial refund based on work completed and documented costs.
  • If a purchased service cannot be delivered as agreed, customers may qualify for a full or partial refund.
  • Completed and accepted services are generally nonrefundable, except where required by law or the service agreement.

3. Subscription Cancellations

Customers may cancel recurring services by submitting a written request before their next billing date.

Cancellation prevents future recurring charges after the effective cancellation date. Customers retain access to paid services through the end of their current billing period, unless otherwise specified in their agreement.

Unused portions of subscription periods are generally nonrefundable unless required by law or agreed in writing.

4. Custom Development and Consulting

Custom AI development, software integrations, cybersecurity implementation, and consulting projects may involve dedicated labor and third-party expenses.

If a project is canceled after work begins, SigOps Inc. will review the completed work, applicable costs, and remaining balance to determine any eligible refund.

5. Refund Processing

Customers must submit refund requests within the applicable eligibility period and provide their invoice number, payment date, and reason for the request.

SigOps Inc. will acknowledge refund requests within three business days and generally provide a decision within seven business days.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Funds may take an additional 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the financial institution.

6. Billing Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact SigOps Inc. directly regarding billing concerns, service issues, or unauthorized transactions.

We will investigate reported concerns and work toward a reasonable resolution in accordance with the service agreement and applicable law.

Nothing in this policy limits customers' rights to dispute transactions through their card issuer.
